How it works

Four simple steps to bring a lost belonging back to its owner.

1. Create an account

Register with your ID number and email. It takes less than a minute.

2. Report an item

Lost something or found something? Fill in the short form and add a photo if you have one.

3. Search and claim

Browse the reports, filter by category, and send a message to the person who posted the item.

Frequently asked questions

Who can see my contact details?

Only users who are logged in can see the contact information on a report.

Can I change or delete my report?

Yes. Open your dashboard to edit, mark as returned, or delete any of your reports.

What happens when someone claims my item?

You see the claim on your dashboard and can approve or reject it. Approving a claim marks the report as returned.
